# Personas

## P-001 — Hiring manager / technical recruiter (primary)

### Context

Evaluating Gaurav for a full-time technical product manager or AI product role (LLMs, agents, agentic systems). Usually reviewing multiple candidates in the same sitting; first pass is a fast skim, often on mobile or a second monitor while multitasking.

### Job to be done

Quickly determine whether Gaurav is credible and worth a conversation — verify he's shipped real things, understand what kind of PM he is, and find a low-friction way to reach out or keep him on file.

### Pain

Most portfolio sites are templated and interchangeable; strong candidates get lost in generic presentation. Hard to tell, in the first 10 seconds, whether a claim ("$1.7M contract value," "6+ agentic projects shipped") is substantiated or inflated.

### Current workaround

Skims a resume PDF or LinkedIn profile instead; if the portfolio doesn't clearly beat that experience within a few seconds, they don't dig further.

### Success

Comes away able to state, in their own words, what Gaurav does and why it's differentiated — and takes one concrete action (books a call, sends an email, downloads the resume, or forwards the link to a colleague).

### Constraints

Time-pressured; may be on mobile; may have JavaScript-heavy embeds fail to load on a slow connection; needs core identity/credibility content available even in a degraded state.

## P-002 — Enterprise/consulting prospect (secondary, non-optimized)

### Context

A potential client evaluating Gaurav for contract technical-delivery or AI-deployment work. Not the primary design target for this rebuild (see `docs/10-product/NON_GOALS.md`), but the site should not actively repel this audience — case studies and quantified enterprise outcomes remain visible.

### Job to be done

Assess whether Gaurav can be trusted to own a similar deployment outcome for their organization.

### Pain

Needs evidence of real enterprise-scale delivery, not just side projects.

### Current workaround

Relies on referral or the existing case-study content.

### Success

Finds the case studies credible enough to start a conversation, even though the primary narrative is now framed around the full-time-hire audience.

### Constraints

Secondary priority — do not let this persona's needs override P-001's information architecture or visual thesis.
